# Leroy campaign opening-page concepts

Preview path: `/concepts/index.html`. Optional query: `?direction=original`, `?direction=office`, or `?direction=playful`.

This is an independent visual study for the opening portion of the mailing landing page, with original layouts and copy. Existing inputs are limited to CTWP branding/contact details, the public repository’s University Dental example and two clearly labeled fictional examples, and the repository's Leroy portrait. The existing landing page and its conversion/tracking behavior are unchanged.

## Three directions

1. **The local original:** editorial split, warm neutrals, original repository portrait. “Less printer drama. More patient time.”
2. **In good company:** image-first composition, sage panel, photoreal AI scene. “Good care starts behind the scenes.”
3. **Office personality:** poster composition, terracotta and cream, imaginative Leroy campaign art. “Big on service. Small on paws.”

The review toolbar switches directions and business names. Recipient logo treatment is available for University Dental Associates using its existing campaign asset; other businesses fall back to names. Verify logo currency before a customer launch.

## Functional scope

Responsive hero, three workflow benefits, phone link, and a three-question local demo with back/restart. No form submission, analytics, CRM writes, inventory matching, live pricing, or automated proposals. The answers live only in JavaScript memory in the current tab. This study does not claim savings, service response times, or specific equipment availability.

## Images

- `public/leroy.png` is the source reference for `public/concepts/assets/leroy-original.webp`. Its provenance is the campaign repository; the seven newly uploaded private Supabase images were not downloaded because the connector cannot retrieve private storage objects and the browser needed sign-in.
- `leroy-office.webp`: generated using built-in image generation with the repository portrait as reference. Prompt: preserve Leroy's terrier identity, black back, gray/tan face, floppy ears, blue bandana; place naturally in a warm oak/cream dental reception with an unbranded, softly blurred copier; editorial daylight, portrait composition, no text or people.
- `leroy-playful.webp`: generated using built-in image generation with the same reference. Prompt: recognizable Leroy in an open white coat and blue bandana, beside paper and an unbranded printer, warm terracotta studio, tactile stylized advertising art, portrait composition, no text or logos.

Generated offices and equipment are illustrative. No AI image represents the actual recipient's premises or an available equipment SKU. WebP encoding is used to reduce page weight.
